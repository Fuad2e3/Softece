About Softece

A small team that takes
the whole stack seriously.

We started in 2017 fixing other people’s broken deployments. That is still how we think — build it so it does not break, and be there when it does.

Our story

From three freelancers
to a full product studio.

Softece began as three engineers taking on the jobs other agencies had abandoned: the app that crashed on launch, the API nobody could document, the server that only one person knew how to restart.

Nine years later we are thirty-eight people across engineering, design and infrastructure — and we still take those jobs. The difference is that now we usually get to build the thing properly from the start.

We deliberately stay small enough that a senior engineer is on every project, and structured enough that nothing depends on one person’s memory.

What we believe

Four rules we don’t bend.

Own your stack

Code, servers, domains and credentials are registered in your name from day one. No lock-in, ever.

Honest timelines

We would rather quote four weeks and deliver in four than promise two and disappear for six.

Boring technology

We pick tools your next developer will recognise. Novelty is a cost you pay for years.

Measure, then optimise

No performance claim without a number behind it. We profile before we rewrite anything.
